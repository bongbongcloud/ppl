Weekly totals & how to run it

  • Per muscle, per week: back 11 sets, hamstrings 10, quads 9, chest 8, delts 7. That sits in the productive band with room to recover on a deficit.
  • Order is A → B → C, not Mon–Wed–Fri. Whenever you next get to the gym, you do the next letter. Missing a week doesn't break anything.
  • Only two sessions that week? Just carry on with the next two letters. Over a month it evens out.
  • Anchors first, always. Short on time, cut the last superset. Never an anchor.
  • Progress anchors by load — hit the top of the rep range on all three sets, then add 2.5 kg (barbell) or the next dumbbell pair. Accessories progress by reps only.
  • Deload every 6–8 weeks: same lifts, same loads, drop to two sets of everything for one week.
  • While cutting, holding your anchor loads steady as bodyweight falls is the win. It looks like a plateau on paper and isn't one.
